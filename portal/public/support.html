Shifty Support

Help & contact for the Shifty app

Shifty helps shift workers plan sleep, wellbeing, and daily routines around their shifts, and find member discounts at local partners. If you need help, we're happy to assist.

Get in touch

Email us any time at contact@shiftyapp.org — we aim to reply within a couple of business days. Please include your account email and a short description of the issue.

Common questions

How do the recommendations work?
Shifty looks at the shifts you enter and uses circadian-rhythm science to suggest when to sleep, get light, have your last coffee, wind down, and more — tailored to each shift type. This is general wellbeing guidance, not medical advice.

How do I add a shift?
On the Timeshifter tab, tap the + button (or tap an empty slot on the calendar), choose the shift type and times, and save. You can repeat a shift across several days too.

How do Shifty Partners work?
Partners are businesses offering member discounts. Open the Shifty Partners tab, show your member card in-store, or tap a partner for directions or their website. Deals can change daily.

Is my data private?
Your account and shifts are stored securely, and only you can access your own information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete my account?
Open the More tab and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, shifts, and profile.

I didn't get my password reset email.
Check your spam/junk folder. If it still hasn't arrived, email us at contact@shiftyapp.org and we'll help.

Important note

Shifty provides general wellbeing information based on published research. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.
